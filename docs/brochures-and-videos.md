# Broker brochures and development videos

## Brochures

Every signed-in staff role can open Broker workspace → PDF brochures. Select 1–20 published developments, optionally starting from a collection owned by that broker. Add a cover headline (100 characters), introduction (1,600 characters), and per-development notes (1,600 characters). Reorder homes inside each note panel. Generate a PDF, inspect the preview, then download or share the file. A WhatsApp number is required so enquiry links have a working broker contact.

The brochure follows the supplied PCL one-pager: solid Midnight (#131E29) pages, Sand (#E0C6AD) accents, white type, Source Sans Light/Semibold and SIL-licensed Source Serif 4 Light Italic. It has one opening page combining the cover photograph, introduction, optional London overview map and compact broker details, followed by exactly one page per development. The viewing CTA and full broker contact card sit underneath the final development; there is no separate map, summary or contact page. The photographic headline fades into Midnight; other panels have hard edges and the map band has thin Sand dividers. The official self-hosted JLL negative lockup appears in the footer. Map pins and development pages use the same numbering and display names; SEVEN and Opus are normalised for display without renaming records or changing their IDs.

The broker's job title comes automatically from My profile and cannot be overridden per brochure. A missing-title prompt links to My profile without discarding the in-tab brochure draft. The compact cover contact and final-development contact card use the current photo/initials, name, title, WhatsApp and work email; the WhatsApp label, telephone number and email have wa.me, tel: and mailto: PDF annotations. Public map broker cards continue to omit email. Brochure assets can render approved development-logo PNGs when supplied; the portfolio currently has no development-logo assets, so no developer marks are invented or inferred from photographs.

Each development shows its saved overall starting price and separate confirmed bedroom starting prices. Unconfirmed prices use “From £[ ]”, never the aggregate price for another bedroom type. Development titles, descriptions, features and broker-note headings are white; location/postcode and price figures use Sand. Prices are read from the current portfolio; no values are copied from the style-reference image or hard-coded. Short introductions use a substantive paragraph from the listing rather than its initial contact/booking prompt. Full descriptions remain available through the linked portfolio. At most six features and the available broker-note space fit one page; shortened copy is explicitly flagged before sharing and the original input stays in the saved draft.

PDFs are generated on the device with jsPDF and bundled SIL-licensed fonts. Choose the shared default London cover or a selected development image. The bundled default is the supplied skyline photograph, self-hosted at /images/london-skyline-cover.jpg. Admins can replace the team default with approved JPEG/PNG/WebP photography. The browser composites the hero-image fade at export time. It loads the first available development photo from up to three candidate URLs, rather than downloading an unused gallery.

“Include a location map” is on for new brochures and stored with the private draft. The browser renders the same key-free OpenFreeMap Positron vector basemap as the portfolio, with brochure-specific pale land, white roads, pale-blue water and green parks. The cover map keeps a wider central-London context around Zones 1 and 2 even for a single selected home, and extends to include any selected outer development. This is a geographic overview, not a plotted fare-zone boundary. It uses numbered Space pins rather than floating development-name cards. Pins are drawn as PDF vectors with clickable development links and collision-aware positioning. A displaced crowded pin has a leader to its true location. Major area labels and map pins are crisp at print scale; secondary manual area labels are omitted to keep the wider overview clear. Only the small OpenStreetMap credit sits inside the map; complete provider credits are retained below the map. No screenshot service or new API account is used.

A map-load error blocks that generation with retry/omit guidance; it never inserts a fabricated or blank map. Brokers can explicitly turn the map off and generate the rest. Failed generation leaves the prior saved PDF intact. There are N+1 pages, with or without the map, for 1–20 developments. The last development has a shorter photographic header and a bounded content area above its CTA; shortened features or notes are explicitly flagged. Existing saved PDFs keep their original layout until regenerated.

Save brochure stores its private settings in the workspace. Generate & save PDF also stores the generated PDF when it is at most 3.5 MB. Each broker can retain up to 50 brochures and 100 MB of PDFs. Larger PDFs remain downloadable in the current tab; settings are saved but the UI explicitly reports that the file was not stored. Saved settings and files survive refresh, logout and deployment. A PDF is a dated snapshot: regenerate it for current stock, prices and broker details. Changing saved settings invalidates its older PDF. Version checks reject concurrent overwrites.

Metadata is held in the existing private portfolio object; binaries are separate objects in `jll-brochure-files`. Production downloads require an active session belonging to the brochure owner. Neither buyer pages nor other brokers expose these private files. Local development uses separate SQLite metadata and private files under DATA_DIR/brochure-files. Remove brochure deletes its metadata and associated binary; downloaded copies remain outside the app.

Outlook draft · PDF attached downloads an RFC 822 `.eml` draft with `X-Unsent: 1`, a UTF-8 body and the actual PDF MIME attachment. Open it in Outlook, enter the recipient and review before sending. Outlook editions may handle draft opening differently; the separate Download PDF action remains available. No mailbox connection is required and no email is sent automatically. WhatsApp opens the sharing service and downloads the PDF for manual attachment. Share file uses supported device share sheets.

Photo loading is limited to seven seconds per image. Missing or cross-origin-blocked images produce placeholders and a visible warning. No scrape or source text is invented to fill missing bedroom prices.

## Videos

Development details support direct HTTPS MP4/WebM/OGG/M4V files and allowlisted YouTube, Vimeo, Brightcove player and bcove.video URLs. Players load only after a buyer clicks Play. Preview cards use YouTube thumbnails where available, with the development cover photo and a local illustration as fallbacks. Direct video players also receive a poster image. YouTube watch, short and embed URLs are normalised to privacy-enhanced embeds. Vimeo unlisted hashes are preserved. Brightcove autoplay parameters are stripped. Unknown providers receive an explicit original-video fallback; arbitrary pages and scripts are never inserted as iframe HTML.

Provider-owned playback restrictions, regional rules or disabled embedding can still stop a video. The original-video link remains available. Live playback and native share-sheet checks require a browser/device; the current app settings disable browser access.

## Validation

Regression tests cover all bundled development video URLs, unsafe URL rejection, provider URL normalisation, PDF pagination, empty selections, filenames and broker/collection link attribution. Sample PDF pages were rendered with Poppler and inspected, including a 20-development long-notes case. Text margins and PDF link annotations were checked with pdfplumber and pypdf. QA artifacts remain in tmp/pdfs and are not committed.

## Saved circular broker photos

Manage → Your broker photo accepts JPEG, PNG and WebP uploads up to 12 MB. The broker can position and zoom the circular preview before saving. The browser decodes the image, removes source metadata through a canvas redraw, and submits a 384 × 384 JPEG. Only the currently signed-in profile can be changed. The server rejects external image URLs, SVG, invalid JPEGs, images above 180,000 data-URL characters, and non-square or excessive dimensions. Portraits are stored with the staff profile, displayed as circles on buyer links, and clipped with a true circular PDF clipping path on the cover and contact page. Removing a photo affects future brochures; existing downloaded PDFs remain unchanged.

Photo edits invalidate an already generated in-tab PDF so it must be regenerated. The original reference’s person, phone numbers, institutional copy and static map are not copied into generated brochures. They use the sharing broker and selected developments. Photo-grid tiles link to the live map instead. Browser interaction checks remain unavailable under the current app settings; PDF pages were rendered and visually reviewed, with pixel-level checks confirming all four portrait corners are clipped away.

## A4 verification

A 20-development stress brochure with long names, six long features and maximum-length notes has 21 pages (combined opening page and 20 development pages, with the CTA below the final development). PDF annotations confirm that each development appears on exactly one dedicated page. Poppler rendering and pdfplumber checks confirm readable layouts with all text inside A4 boundaries. The portfolio title is London New Homes Portfolio and broker-name text is explicitly dark on the green contact button in both themes.
